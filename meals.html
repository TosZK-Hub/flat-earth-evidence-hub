Dinner for one

Eight simple one-portion dinners using staples you'll find at Coles, Woolworths or Aldi. No prices listed — they change weekly. Follow food-safety guidance on packs; check allergens yourself.

Fried rice for one

You need: 1 cup cooked (leftover) rice, 1 egg, ½ cup frozen mixed veg, 1 spring onion, soy sauce

Scramble egg in a hot pan, add veg, then rice and soy; toss 3–4 min.

Tuna pasta

You need: 80 g dry pasta, 1 small (95 g) tin tuna, ½ cup passata, handful baby spinach

Cook pasta; warm passata with tuna, stir through spinach and pasta.

Egg & veg wrap

You need: 2 eggs, 1 wholemeal wrap, grated cheese, tomato, handful salad leaves

Scramble eggs, fill wrap with eggs, cheese, tomato and leaves.

One-pan chicken thigh & veg

You need: 1 chicken thigh fillet, 1 small potato, ½ zucchini, olive oil, dried mixed herbs

Dice everything, toss in oil and herbs, roast 200°C ~30 min until chicken is cooked through.

Microwave baked potato

You need: 1 large potato, ¼ tin baked beans or 4-bean mix, grated cheese

Prick potato, microwave 6–8 min until soft, top with warmed beans and cheese.

Lentil & tomato soup

You need: ½ tin brown lentils, ½ tin diced tomatoes, ½ onion, 1 cup stock, cumin

Soften onion, add rest, simmer 10 min. Freeze any extra.

Pesto gnocchi

You need: 200 g shelf gnocchi, 1 tbsp jar pesto, cherry tomatoes, parmesan

Boil gnocchi till they float, toss with pesto and halved tomatoes.

Salmon (tinned) rice bowl

You need: 1 small tin salmon, 1 microwave rice cup, cucumber, frozen edamame, soy/kewpie-style mayo

Heat rice, top with salmon, sliced cucumber, thawed edamame, dressing.
