Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what information Kleinspan ("we") collects when you use this website, and how it's handled. We aim to comply with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) where they apply to us.

What we collect on this site

  • No accounts, no tracking cookies, no analytics. This site doesn't use advertising or analytics scripts.
  • Theme setting. If you switch between light and dark mode, that choice is saved in your own browser (local storage). It never leaves your device.
  • Hosting logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address for security and operations. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

When you buy

Payments are handled by Stripe. Purchases are processed through Stripe's Managed Payments service, where Link (by Stripe) is the merchant of record and handles your payment details, receipts and tax. We don't see or store your full card number. We receive your name, email address and order details so we can deliver your files and help you with your order. See Stripe's privacy policy.

Email updates (optional)

If we offer an email sign-up and you choose to join, we'll use your email address only to send Kleinspan news and new packs. You can unsubscribe at any time. The sign-up provider will be named here before it goes live.

Your choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact: contact details coming soon.

Children

Our printables are for children, but this website is for parents and carers. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Complaints

If you're not happy with how we've handled your information, contact us first. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
