Nuut · Leer Afrikaans met Hettie

From hallo to real conversations, one level at a time.

Short lessons in your browser, every word and sentence spoken in Afrikaans, with streaks and progress that sync across your devices. Your coach is Hettie Hadida: South Africa’s loudest alarm clock, now shouting at you to do your lesson.

  • Beginner (free): greetings, family, food, colours and numbers
  • Graad 1 to 12 (Kleinspan Plus, coming soon): school-style grades modelled on CAPS FAL, with reading, writing, grammar, idioms and real conversations. The first unit of Graad 1 is a free taster.

How it works

  1. Druk · print

    Download the PDF and print on thick paper or card at home, the library or the office (we won’t tell).

  2. Knip · cut

    Dotted lines show you where. Print double-sided and each back lines up with its front.

  3. Speel · play

    Snap, memory, treasure hunts. The how-to page has four games that take no prep at all.

A note from us

We’re an Afrikaans family living abroad. We wanted Afrikaans to stay part of everyday life, for us and for the partners and friends who are learning it, and to feel like play, not homework.

We couldn’t find flashcards with the words we grew up with, in a style we’d want on our own fridge. So we made them, and then a course to go with them.

Jesse
