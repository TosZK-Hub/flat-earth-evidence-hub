Solo Kit AU

A practical toolkit for Australians living alone. Rent, power and internet don't halve just because there's one of you. Roughly one in four Australian households is a lone-person household, and that number is projected to grow.

Sources: ABS Household and Family Projections (lone-person households ~26% of households; 3.4–4.0 million people projected to live alone by 2046) · Finder Singles Tax Report 2026 (finder.com.au).
