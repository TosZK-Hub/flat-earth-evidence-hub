How we label claims

Full method →

Every proof page carries three labels plus a compare strip. We steelman FE and still refuse to pass speculation as settled science.

Established evidence

Multi-method consensus measurements, primary treaties, documented geodesy & image pipelines — including globe-model results that conflict with an FE claim.

Well-argued hypothesis

FE models and contested readings of observations with named proponents and real critics.

Speculation / metaphor

Dome builders, hidden controllers, motive-only stories — clearly marked, never sold as fact.
