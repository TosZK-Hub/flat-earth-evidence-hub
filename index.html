Online classroom · flat-earth teacher · devil’s-advocate

Learn to weigh flat-Earth claims like a careful student

This is a sequenced course, not a slogan dump. Your teacher steelmans the strongest FE community arguments with named sources, then shows where globe-model measurements conflict — labelled established, hypothesis, or speculation. Flat Earth is not presented as proven science.

Established evidence

Replicated / multi-method consensus measurements, primary treaties, documented pipelines — including globe results that conflict with an FE claim.

Well-argued hypothesis

FE models and contested readings with named proponents and real critics.

Speculation / metaphor

Motive-only stories, unnamed “they,” unverified anecdotes — marked, never sold as fact.
