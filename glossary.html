Neutral definitions

Glossary

Terms as used in FE community arguments and in this hub’s labels. Definitions here are descriptive, not endorsements. Linked proofs carry the steelman + falsifiers.

AE map (azimuthal equidistant)
A map projection of a sphere that preserves distance and direction from a chosen centre. North-polar AE charts are the usual “flat Earth map” poster shape. See AE / Gleason proof.
Bedford Level
Straight drainage canal (Old Bedford River, England) used by Rowbotham and later experimenters for water-surface sights. See Bedford proof.
Blue Marble
Family of whole-Earth images; modern NASA products are often multi-instrument composites / visualizations (e.g. 2002 MODIS mosaic), distinct from Apollo film. See imagery proof.
CEAI
Informal community acronym sometimes expanded along the lines of “commonly accepted… / controlled… education / information” — used as a catch-all for mainstream teaching. On this site: treat as speculation / rhetoric unless a specific named curriculum or agency document is cited.
Curvature approximation (8″ × mi²)
Popular rule of thumb for geometric drop: about 8 inches times miles squared, from d²/(2R) with mean Earth radius. Incomplete without observer height and refraction. Use the horizon calculator.
Firmament
In some FE (often biblical) models, a dome or expanse over the plane. Labelled hypothesis / speculation on this hub unless tied to a checkable measurement claim.
Gleason map
Alexander Gleason’s 1892 circular New Standard Map of the World with time-dial apparatus (US patent 497,917). Widely reprinted in FE media. Maps proof.
Hull-down
Nautical: a ship’s hull hidden while masts remain visible — classically cited for curvature; FE replies emphasise perspective and zoom. Horizon proof.
Ice wall
FE reading of Antarctica as a perimeter ice barrier enclosing a disc ocean, often tied to Antarctic Treaty travel rules. Antarctica proof.
Perspective
Geometric apparent size/height change with distance; used in FE arguments to explain disappearances without curvature. Globe accounts treat perspective and curvature as both relevant.
Plane / flat Earth
Model family in which Earth’s surface is treated as locally or globally planar (with variants: monopole AE disc, bipolar, etc.), rather than an oblate spheroid. This hub steelmans arguments; it does not call the model proven science.
Refraction (atmospheric)
Bending of light through air layers of different density/temperature. Strong near water; can lift or distort distant targets. Established optics; central to Bedford and laser debates.
Spotlight sun
FE hypothesis that a local sun illuminates a disc like a spotlight, explaining day/night without a globe. Hypothesis — needs risky predictions (angular size, parallel rays, seasons).
Theodolite
Precision instrument for measuring horizontal and vertical angles; used in historic Bedford/Wallace-style surveys and modern recreations.
Zetetic
Rowbotham’s term for inquiry from sensory evidence and experiment (“Zetetic Astronomy”). Historical method label inside FE literature — not a synonym for “proven.”
