    Tips for a clean read
    • Plain background, whole card in view: the frame turns green when Holowise has found the card edges.
    • Tilt, don't flatten: angle the card 10–15° away from the light so reflections slide off the number.
    • Sleeves and top-loaders add glare. Slabs work best with Ximilar on.
    • One Piece: set the toggle to One Piece. The code (e.g. OP05-119) is bottom-right.

          About Holowise

          Not financial advice. Prices marked converted come from US/EU marketplaces (TCGplayer and Cardmarket via TCGdex / Pokémon TCG API, TCGplayer via OPTCG API or JustTCG, or Ximilar). They're converted to AUD at ECB reference rates from Frankfurter. They're estimates only, not Australian sales. Prices marked AU sales (only with an AU proxy) are eBay Australia sold prices from TCG Prices AU.

          Signals, projections and deal verdicts are simple statistical estimates from past prices and your calculator settings. They are not forecasts you can rely on. When there isn't enough history, no signal is shown.
