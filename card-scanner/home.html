Should I grade it?

Published Australian grading routes (PSA, BGS and CGC through local submitters, plus PCG) with GST, return post and customs. Add your own grade odds to see the breakeven. Includes a centering check from your photo.

🎪

Show mode

Save your cards' prices before a show and scan with no signal. Batch-scan a binder for a running total and a buy offer, or go hands-free: scan, hear the price and say the asking price.

Honest numbers

Prices come from public market data: TCGdex (Cardmarket and TCGplayer), the Pokémon TCG API, the OPTCG API and JustTCG, converted to AUD at European Central Bank reference rates. Australian eBay sold data is shown only where Holowise has a licensed source, and is labelled "AU sales". Hold, Sell and Watch signals are simple statistics from past prices. When there isn't enough history, Holowise shows no signal instead of guessing. None of it is financial advice.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Scanning, AUD estimates, deal checks and a 100-card collection are free with no account. Pro and Dealer add the selling, grading, history and show tools.

Are the prices Australian sales?

Mostly not yet. The free sources are US and EU marketplaces converted to AUD, and they're labelled "converted". Each card links to eBay Australia sold listings so you can check local sales yourself. Australian sold data appears, labelled "AU sales", only where Holowise has a licensed source.

Is this financial advice?

No. Prices, signals, projections and deal verdicts are estimates from past data and your own settings. Card prices can move fast. Check recent sales and make your own decision.

Which cards does it support?

English Pokémon and One Piece cards. Japanese prints, graded slabs and damaged cards can be worth very different amounts, so the app tells you which variant and condition a price is for.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, in Safari or Chrome. Open the app and choose Add to Home Screen to use it like an app. The camera needs permission the first time.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. Your collection, watchlist and settings stay in your browser's storage, and there's no Holowise account. See the privacy policy for the services the app contacts to look up prices.

How do I pay, cancel or get a refund?

When checkout opens, payments will be handled by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the seller of record and handles GST. Prices are shown in AUD, but Lemon Squeezy processes payments in US dollars at the mid-market rate, so your bank statement may show a USD amount or a foreign transaction fee. Your licence key arrives by email. Cancel any time from the link in your receipt email. Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law always apply. See the terms.

Do you earn from eBay links?

Some eBay links may become affiliate links through the eBay Partner Network. If you buy after clicking one, Holowise may earn a small commission at no cost to you. It never changes prices, verdicts or which selling option shows as best. The app says so next to those links whenever it's switched on.

What about smart glasses?

Hands-free mode already works on a phone in a chest mount. Native glasses support will follow once the glasses platforms open paid app stores in Australia.
