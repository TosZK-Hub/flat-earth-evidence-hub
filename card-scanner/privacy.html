Who we are

Holowise makes the Holowise card scanner web app. Contact: [contact email to be added before launch].

The short version

  • There's no Holowise account. Your collection, watchlist, settings and any keys you enter are stored in your browser on your device.
  • Card photos are read on your device. They leave it only if you turn on Ximilar recognition (see below).
  • To look up prices, the app sends card names and numbers (not your identity) to the price services listed below.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies of our own.

What stays on your device

Your collection, watchlist, price snapshots, calculator and signal settings, offline show packs, plan status and licence key are stored in your browser's local storage and IndexedDB. You can download a backup or erase everything under Settings → Data. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data also removes it.

Payments

Paid plans are sold by Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy collects your name, email, payment and billing details to process the purchase, and sends your licence key. We receive your order details, including your email, from Lemon Squeezy so we can support your subscription. We never see your full card number.

Launch updates

If you sign up for launch updates, we'll use your email only to send Holowise news, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Camera and microphone

The app asks for camera access to scan cards and microphone access only for hands-free mode. You can turn either off in your browser or phone settings at any time.

Children

Holowise is not directed at children under 13. Paid plans require the purchaser to be 18 or older, or to have a parent or guardian's permission.

Access, correction and complaints

Most of your data is only on your device, so you control it directly. For anything we hold, such as order emails or launch sign-ups, contact us to access, correct or delete it. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and note significant changes in the app.
