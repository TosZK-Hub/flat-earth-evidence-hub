About · methodology · classroom rules

How this classroom grades evidence

Think of this page as the grading rubric on the wall. Same rules apply in every lesson.

Mission

This is a devil’s-advocate flat-Earth classroom: a sequenced course that steelmans the strongest FE community proofs, videos and arguments — with named sources — beside established globe-model measurements where those conflict. Published by Jesse Hobbs from Brisbane. Live on GitHub Pages.

We do not present flat Earth as proven science. We also do not sneer. Readers should leave better informed about what the community actually argues and what multi-method geodesy and optics currently say.

How the classroom is organised

Competing FE hubs often win on volume (endless proof lists), wiki bulk, or forum energy. This classroom aims to win on sequence and honesty — without hype lies:

  • Course sequence: syllabus, modules, lessons, labs, quizzes, local progress (feh-course).
  • Organization: themes, ranked “strongest arguments,” client search, research library behind the lessons.
  • Steelman + falsifiers: every deep dive carries a three-column compare card — FE steelman | Globe-model account | What would settle it.
  • Interactive tools: FE-trap claim tester; on-site horizon/distance estimator with formula, assumptions, and dual labels (globe calc = established geometry; FE “no drop” = hypothesis).
  • Citations: primary links (treaties, NASA blogs, patent/UN text, verified YouTube IDs). Cite more, claim less.
  • Labels: established / hypothesis / speculation on every theme — rivals that assert FE as settled science fail this bar.

See competition-notes.md for the survey of wiki.tfes.org, Dubay lists, IFERS, FlatEarth.ws, curvature apps, and forums. We do not plagiarize their copy.

Editorial line

  • Steelmanning FE: state the community’s best case in its own terms before answering.
  • Name actors: Samuel Rowbotham, Alfred Russel Wallace, Mark Sargent, Flat Earth UK, Eric Dubay, Alexander Gleason, NASA’s Robert Simmon, the Antarctic Treaty Secretariat, the United Nations — never vague “they.”
  • Tone: community-useful, curious, direct. Depth over hype.

Evidence standards

  1. Read before citing. Numbers and quotations checked against primary sources on publication day.
  2. Prefer resolving links. Treaties, papers, official pages, named YouTube watch URLs.
  3. Verify video IDs. No fabricated embeds. If a URL fails verification, it is cut.
  4. Critics in the same post. FE claims carry the strongest published counter-account.
  5. Unverifiable means cut or labelled speculation.

The three-label system

Established evidence

Multi-method consensus measurements (surveys allowing for refraction, satellite geodesy, lunar eclipse geometry as modelled on a globe, primary treaty text, documented image-processing pipelines, standard map-projection definitions). “Established” means strong evidence, not closed forever.

Well-argued hypothesis

FE models and contested readings (e.g. low-telescope canal sights as flatness; AE disc as physical map; spotlight sun). Real support inside the community, real critics outside.

Speculation / metaphor

Hidden dome builders, motive-only NASA cabals, unverified “turned away at gunpoint” anecdotes without named primary records. Never passed off as fact.

Every proof page ends with: What is established / What is hypothesis / What remains open.

How videos are chosen

  1. Widely cited inside the FE community or high-quality educational counter-material.
  2. Official YouTube watch URL verified before publish.
  3. Labelled: FE community · Critical viewing · Globe-model education.
  4. Behind the Curve appears as critical viewing (globe-leaning documentary featuring community members), not as an FE manifesto.
  5. Long proof-list videos (e.g. Dubay) are hosted as community primaries — not as 200 established facts.

What this site is NOT

  • Not a cult, church, or loyalty test.
  • Not a claim that flat Earth is proven science.
  • Not health, financial or legal advice.
  • Not a call to illegal action or to harass scientists, pilots, Antarctic crews or tourists.
  • Not anonymous “they” conspiracy content without primary documents.
