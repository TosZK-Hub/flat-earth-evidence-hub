About Kleinspan

Kleinspan is what Afrikaans speakers call the little ones in the family.

Kleinspan is made by an Afrikaans family living abroad. When you raise kids far from South Africa, English takes over fast: at day care, at school, on the telly. We wanted simple, good-looking printables that make it easy to keep Afrikaans in the house, without screens and without fuss.

What we make

Flashcards, posters and activity pages in Afrikaans with English alongside, so parents who are still brushing up can join in too. Everything is designed from scratch: big clear letters, bright colours and simple shapes. No borrowed cartoon characters.

Who it's for

  • South African families in Australia, New Zealand, the UK and anywhere else
  • Grandparents who want to send something special to the grandkids overseas
  • Mixed-language families where one parent speaks Afrikaans
