About Solo Kit AU

Solo Kit AU is a practical kit for renters and owners living alone in Australia: a cost estimator, checklists, meals for one and gear categories. It's not dating advice and not health, financial or legal advice.

Methodology

The estimator is a simple 50/50 split of the costs you enter; it runs entirely in your browser. Figures we quote are linked to their source. We don't invent prices, stats or product reviews.

Sources

Sources: ABS Household and Family Projections (lone-person households ~26% of households; 3.4–4.0 million people projected to live alone by 2046) · Finder Singles Tax Report 2026 (finder.com.au).
