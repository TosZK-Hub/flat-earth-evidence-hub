Claim checklist

Not advice

An evaluation aid only. It does not decide Earth’s shape, guilt or policy. Results are a local score against this hub’s bar — re-check the sources yourself. No health, financial or legal advice.

Suggested practice claims

  • “Rowbotham’s 1838 telescope-over-water sight proves zero curvature for six miles.”
  • “Zooming a ship back into frame falsifies hull-down curvature.”
  • “The Antarctic Treaty forbids travel past an ice wall.”
  • “Blue Marble images are Photoshopped composites, therefore space photography is fake.”
  • “Gleason’s patented map proves the Patent Office recognised a flat Earth.”
  • “One unreplicated lake laser test settles geometric drop.”

Run each through the checklist after reading the matching proof page. For distances, open the horizon calculator.
