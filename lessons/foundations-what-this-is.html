What this course is — and isn’t

In this lesson I tell you the contract: devil’s-advocate FE info hub, community-useful, curious, and allergic to loyalty tests.

What this course is

  • A sequenced classroom that steelmans strong FE community arguments with named sources and verified videos.
  • A place that also carries globe-model accounts and falsifiers beside those claims.
  • Static HTML you can read offline; progress stays on your device.
  • Published framing by Jesse Hobbs (Brisbane) — methodology over hype.
Well-argued hypothesis (course stance)

Presenting FE arguments at their strongest helps readers weigh them; it does not convert those arguments into established science.

What this course is NOT

  • Not a claim that flat Earth is proven science.
  • Not a cult, church, or loyalty test.
  • Not health, financial, or legal advice.
  • Not a call to illegal action or to harass scientists, pilots, Antarctic crews, or tourists.
  • Not anonymous “they” content without primary documents.
Speculation we refuse to launder

Motive-only NASA cabals, dome-builder myths, and gunpoint travel myths without named records stay labelled speculation or are cut.

How videos are chosen

Only verified YouTube watch URLs already checked for this hub. Labels: FE community · Critical viewing · Globe-model education. Long proof-list videos (e.g. Eric Dubay) are community primaries — not 200 established facts.
