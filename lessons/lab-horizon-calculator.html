Lab: horizon calculator

In this lab you run the same numbers two ways: globe geometric drop (established spherical geometry with stated assumptions) versus FE “no drop” (hypothesis). The calculator does not settle the Earth model by itself — it makes hidden assumptions visible.

Before you click

  • Globe drop uses a standard Earth-radius approximation; refraction is not fully modelled here — treat optical field tests separately (see Bedford & horizon lessons).
  • FE “no drop” is the community baseline that standing water / Earth is a plane over the entered distance.
  • Log eye height and distance when you compare to a video claim.
Established vs hypothesis. The globe calculator output is established geometry under its assumptions. The FE zero-drop line is a well-argued hypothesis inside the community, not a multi-method consensus measurement.
