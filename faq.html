Frequently asked questions

What age are the packs for?

They're designed for toddlers and young kids who are starting to learn letters, numbers, colours and shapes, roughly preschool and early school age. Older kids learning Afrikaans as a second language can use them too.

What exactly do I get?

A PDF file sized for A4 paper. The alphabet pack has 26 cards, the numbers pack has 20 cards with ten-frames, and the colours and shapes pack has two posters plus a colour-in sheet. The bundle has all three.

How do I print them?

Print at 100% or "actual size" on A4. Card stock or thick paper (160–200 gsm) lasts longest. Cut along the grey dashed lines, and laminate if you like.

I live in the US and use Letter paper. Will it work?

Yes. Choose "fit to page" in your print settings and the cards will shrink slightly to fit.

Do you post printed copies?

No, everything is a digital download. That keeps prices low and means you can print a fresh set whenever a card goes missing behind the couch.

Can I use them in my classroom or playgroup?

Yes, in one classroom or group. Please don't upload the files anywhere or share them with other teachers; send them the link instead.

Why is there an English word on every card?

So that parents and grandparents who are rusty, or still learning, can join in. Many of our families are mixed-language.

What about refunds?

Because these are instant digital downloads, we generally don't offer change-of-mind refunds. But if a file is faulty or isn't what was described, contact us and we'll fix it or refund you, as required under Australian Consumer Law.
