# Competition notes — Flat Earth Evidence Hub

Survey date: 2026-11-05 (Australia/Brisbane). Do not plagiarize competitor copy; steelman their features, leapfrog their gaps.

## Competitors surveyed

### 1. The Flat Earth Wiki (wiki.tfes.org)
**Does well:** Deep terminology, Experimental Evidence index (Bedford, Bishop, laser lists), map catalogue (monopole / bipolar), FAQ framing from first principles.  
**Gap we leapfrog:** Asserts FE as demonstrated truth; little steelman of globe geodesy; no interactive claim-tester or side-by-side falsifiers; wiki UX is dense forum-era HTML.

### 2. Flat Earth Society forum (forum.tfes.org)
**Does well:** Active boards (Theory, Investigations, Media), long-running debate archive, project threads.  
**Gap:** Discussion, not a curated evidence desk. Hard to find ranked, labelled proofs. No progress UI or calculators.

### 3. Eric Dubay — “200 Proofs” (ericdubay.wordpress.com + viral video)
**Does well:** Massive enumerated claim list, multilingual PDFs, high discoverability on YouTube.  
**Gap:** Volume over methodology; many items mix anecdote, misapplied 8″×mi² slogans, and motive stories without primary checks. We refuse to copy the list wholesale; we organise fewer themes with labels + falsifiers.

### 4. IFERS / International Flat Earth Research Society forum
**Does well:** Reference Materials board, ice-wall / Antarctica sections, ebook threads.  
**Gap:** Forum UX; uneven sourcing; conspiracy boards dilute the evidence desk.

### 5. FlatEarth.ws (globe-leaning analysis site)
**Does well:** Curvature-analysis case database, refraction-aware calculator reviews (Bislin, Metabunk), named observation geometry.  
**Gap:** Primarily debunk framing — FE steelman is thin. We keep *both* steelman and established globe geometry on the same page.

### 6. Interactive curvature tools (Walter Bislin Curvature App; Metabunk Curve Calculator; Dizzib ECC)
**Does well:** Best-in-class geometry sims (refraction sliders, FOV). Community often cites these — sometimes without refraction.  
**Gap:** Tools sit outside FE hubs. We ship an on-site horizon/distance estimator with explicit assumptions and dual labels (globe calc = established geometry; FE “no drop” = hypothesis).

### 7. Behind the Curve (film + behindthecurvefilm.com)
**Does well:** Documented community experiments (laser / board tests), sociology of belief.  
**Gap:** Globe-leaning edit; not a proof library. We cite trailer + experiment context under Critical viewing, never as an FE manifesto.

### 8. Discord / Meetup FE communities
**Does well:** Live debate, social discovery.  
**Gap:** Ephemeral; no persistent labelled archive. We stay a static, citable desk.

## Differentiation plan (what we ship to be a step ahead)

| Rival strength | Our leapfrog |
|----------------|--------------|
| Long proof lists (Dubay) | Ranked themes + filters + progress, not 200 unlabelled bullets |
| Wiki depth (TFES) | Side-by-side **FE steelman \| Globe account \| Falsifiers** on every deep dive |
| Forum debate | Claim-tester with FE-specific traps (anecdote vs experiment, unreplicated lasers, photo authenticity, map-projection confusion) |
| External calculators | On-site horizon/distance estimator with formula + assumptions |
| Debunk sites | Dual entry paths: explore FE arguments *or* weigh globe counter-evidence |
| Video dumps | Verified hub with FE / critical / globe tags + “start here” order |
| Jargon walls | Neutral glossary linked to proofs |

## Hard rules we keep (competitors often don’t)
- Never present FE as proven science.
- Cite more, claim less; verify video IDs.
- No anonymous “they”; no health/financial/legal advice.
- Label established / hypothesis / speculation on every theme.
