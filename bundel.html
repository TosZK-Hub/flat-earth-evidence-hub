Four ways to play

From the how-to page included in every pack.

Wys my

Show me

Lay out four cards and say a word. The other player points. Then swap, and get it wrong on purpose now and then.

Geheuespel

Memory game

Print two sets of fronts, shuffle them face down and hunt for pairs. Say each word as it flips.

Skattejag

Treasure hunt

Hide five cards around the house. Each one found gets said out loud or matched to something real.

Woord van die dag

Word of the day

One card on the fridge. Use the word at dinner and in the car. Tomorrow, a new one.
