Blue Marble & Space Imagery Processing Debates

The most shareable “gotcha” in modern FE media is that famous whole-Earth pictures are composites, CGI or Photoshop. That sentence mixes a documented fact (many Blue Marble products are layered data visualizations) with a leap (therefore no photographs of Earth exist / spaceflight is fake). This page separates those layers.

Side-by-side claim card

FE steelman

Iconic “photograph of the whole Earth” images used in schools and phones are often not single-shutter photos. Clouds are stitched, oceans colourised from chlorophyll data, atmospheres painted in compositing software. If agencies openly use Photoshop-class tools, the public has been shown a constructed product — so trust in space photography should drop until raw, continuous, unedited full-disc captures are produced under independent audit.

Hypothesis on authenticity standards; parts of the processing critique track primary NASA blogs.

Globe-model account

Low-Earth-orbit imagers (e.g. Terra/MODIS) cannot see a full sunlit hemisphere in one frame the way Apollo did from translunar distances. Scientists therefore composite many cloud-cleared scenes into a global mosaic, then wrap it on a sphere for communication. Robert Simmon and Reto Stöckli documented the 2002 Blue Marble pipeline publicly. Compositing is standard remote sensing — not a secret confession that Earth is flat.

Established for the processing history; globe shape rests on many other methods besides Blue Marble art.

What would settle it

  • Publish raw instrument granules + reproducible mosaic code for a named Blue Marble release (partially done via Earth Observatory archives).
  • Independent high-altitude / lunar-distance imaging with open shutter logs (distinct from web wallpaper).
  • Separate claims: (A) “this wallpaper is a composite” vs (B) “no Earth curvature / no spaceflight.” (A) can be true while (B) stays speculation.

The FE case (steelmanned)

Community presenters (including Mark Sargent’s widely shared Flat Earth Clues introduction) argue that the public never sees a continuous, uncut, fish-eye-free video of the entire Earth rotating in space from a camera that non-agency parties can audit. They note that modern “Blue Marble” images are assembled, that cloud layers sometimes show clone-tool repetition, and that fish-eye lenses on rockets exaggerate limb curvature. From that, many conclude space agencies are producing theatre rather than exploration.

Well-argued hypothesis (partial)

Demanding open raw data and continuous footage is a fair epistemic standard. Documented compositing does mean many viral whole-Earth images are visualizations, not single photographs.

Speculation

Jumping from “composites exist” to “the planet is a plane and all orbital mechanics are fabricated” without engaging satellite ranging, VLBI, GNSS, lunar laser ranging, or circumnavigation geometry.

What NASA published about the pipeline

In “Crafting the Blue Marble” (NASA Earth Observatory blog, 2011), Robert Simmon describes Reto Stöckli assembling on the order of ~10,000 Terra/MODIS scenes over ~100 days into a ~43,200 × 21,600 pixel cloud-cleared surface map. Simmon then added ocean colour from chlorophyll products, stand-in sea ice, a cloud mosaic stitched from hundreds of scenes, topography for texture, rendered layers in 3D software, and composited atmosphere/cloud plates in Photoshop — including filling orbital gaps near the equator with cloned cloud features he openly calls photoshopped.

The NASA Earth Observatory product page for the 2002 Blue Marble likewise states the image is a seamless mosaic of land, ocean, sea ice and clouds from satellite-based observations — not a claim of one shutter click from deep space.

Established evidence

The modern Blue Marble family includes documented multi-instrument composites and visualizations. Simmon’s blog and Earth Observatory pages are primary for that processing history. Apollo 17’s 1972 whole-disc photograph (AS17-148-22727 and related) is a separate, earlier category: film from translunar distance.

Do not conflate Apollo film with MODIS wallpaper

FE and globe speakers both err when they treat “Blue Marble” as one object. Rough timeline:

  • 1972 Apollo 17 — whole-disc colour photograph from the spacecraft (established historical NASA photo archive item).
  • 2002+ MODIS Blue Marble — data visualization / mosaic family (Simmon, Stöckli, et al.), later used as iPhone wallpaper; Simmon notes he did not know Apple would ship it.

Critiquing 2002 Photoshop layers does not automatically refute 1972 film, and defending 1972 film does not make the 2002 wallpaper a raw photo. Keep the objects distinct.

What is established / hypothesis / open

Established

Many flagship Blue Marble products are multi-layer composites; NASA authors described ocean colour fill, cloud stitching, cloned gap fills, and Photoshop compositing; MODIS mosaics are not Apollo-style single frames.

Hypothesis

That documented visualization pipelines imply intentional deception about Earth’s shape (FE); that every public Earth image without a perfect raw dump is worthless (over-hard globe-side dismissal).

Open / speculation

Agency-wide CGI Earth conspiracy; motive-only stories about why composites exist that ignore the LEO field-of-view constraint Simmon states plainly.
