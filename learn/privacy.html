Privacy notice: Kleinspan Leer (the course app)

Last updated: 22 November 2026. This notice covers the learning app at /learn/. It sits alongside the main Kleinspan site privacy policy.

Children’s data

  • Accounts belong to parents. Only a parent or guardian aged 18 or over can create an account, and they confirm this when signing up. Children never create accounts, sign in, or type an email address.
  • Minimal data about children. For each child profile (up to 3) we store only: a first name or nickname that the parent chooses, an animal avatar, and lesson progress (which lessons were finished, XP earned, whether there were mistakes, and the local date, used for streaks). We do not ask for a child’s surname, birthday, age, school, photo, voice, location or contact details.
  • No ads and no tracking. The app shows no advertising, has no analytics or tracking scripts, no third-party cookies, and no social features. Children can’t chat or share anything with other users.
  • Microphone. The optional “say it” exercise uses your browser’s built-in speech recognition only when the child taps the microphone. We don’t record, receive or store any audio. Depending on the browser, the browser maker (for example Google in Chrome) may process the speech to turn it into text; you can skip the microphone at any time.
  • Parent controls. Parent-only screens sit behind a simple grown-up check. From Parents you can rename or remove a child profile (which deletes that child’s progress), or delete the whole account and everything in it.
  • Guest mode stores progress only on the device (browser local storage) and sends nothing to our server.

What we collect from parents

  • Your email address and a password. The password is never stored: we keep only a salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hash (100,000 iterations).
  • A sign-in token kept on your device. Our server stores only a hash of it, and it expires after 60 days or when you sign out.
  • Your plan status (free or Kleinspan Plus). When paid plans launch, payments will be handled by Stripe; we would store a Stripe customer and subscription reference, never card details.
  • Your IP address is used briefly to rate-limit sign-in attempts and is not stored in our database.

Where it’s stored

Account and progress data is stored with Cloudflare (Workers and D1 database), which may process it outside Australia. The app files are hosted on GitHub Pages.

How we use it

Only to run the app: sign you in, show your children their progress on any device, and (later) manage your subscription. We don’t sell or share personal information and we don’t use it for marketing to children.

How long we keep it

Until you delete a child profile or your account. Deleting is immediate and permanent on our database. Expired sign-in tokens are removed automatically.

Your rights

You can see your children’s data in the app, change it, or delete it at any time. Under the Australian Privacy Principles you can also ask us for access or correction, or make a complaint, using the contact details below. If you’re not satisfied you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
